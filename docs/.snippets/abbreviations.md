*[SMTP]: Simple Mail Transfer Protocol: how the app hands its mail to a mail server, which delivers it
*[STARTTLS]: The connection starts plain and is switched to encryption before anything is sent (port 587)
*[TLS]: Transport Layer Security: the encryption that protects a connection
*[CIDR]: A range of IP addresses written as address/size, for example 192.0.2.0/24 for 192.0.2.0 to 192.0.2.255
*[IANA]: The registry that names time zones, such as Europe/Amsterdam
*[QR]: A square barcode a phone camera can scan to open a link
